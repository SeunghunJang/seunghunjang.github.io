01 · About

From first-principles physics to chemical AI

I am a researcher at the Korea Research Institute of Chemical Technology (KRICT), specializing in chemical AI, quantum-chemical data generation, and data-driven molecular and materials design. My work connects physics-based computation with accessible scientific datasets, machine-learning models, and research platforms.

My earlier research focused on electronic structures, low-dimensional materials, and semiconductor nanostructures. Today, I work across computational chemistry, materials informatics, polymer property prediction, and LLM-assisted molecular design.
